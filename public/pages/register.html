SoundSphere Platform Privacy Policy & Data Protection
SoundSphere Privacy Policy
Effective Date: October 2026  •  Platform Controller: SoundSphere (Denver E. Cabrera: 0951 602 8992 | soundsphere@gmail.com)

SoundSphere is a web-based booking platform connecting clients with professional lights and sounds service providers. This privacy policy explains how your personal data is collected, handled, and protected in accordance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile: Full name, email address, contact number, and profile photo.
  • Provider Application & Verification: Valid government-issued ID, business permit, equipment details, service packages, and relevant business documents submitted for provider approval.
  • Booking & Event Details: Selected equipment/service packages, event dates, event venues/locations, schedule requirements, and in-app text messages between clients and providers.
  • Payments & Billing: Transaction reference numbers, down payments, remaining balances, subscription plan records, and payout recipient details (debit/credit card credentials are processed directly by our secure third-party payment gateway and are not stored by SoundSphere).
  • Reviews & Platform Records: Ratings, event feedback, dispute or non-appearance incident reports, and operational access logs.

2. Purposes and Legal Basis

  • Fulfilling Bookings: To verify schedule availability, confirm reservations, coordinate down payments/balances, and facilitate event delivery.
  • Provider Verification: To authenticate provider identity, assess business permits, prevent marketplace fraud, and approve provider subscription plans.
  • Communication & Support: To enable text messaging between clients and providers, handle inquiries, and review non-appearance or refund claims.
  • Compliance & Security: To protect system integrity and comply with statutory obligations under Section 13 of the Data Privacy Act.

3. Public vs. Private Information

  • Public Content: Approved provider business names, profile photos, equipment lists, service packages, pricing, coverage areas, and client reviews are publicly visible to platform visitors.
  • Between Booking Parties: The client and the booked provider receive necessary contact details, event venue locations, and schedule information strictly to fulfill the service.
  • Confidential: Uploaded government IDs, business permits, provider payout details, transaction disputes, and private incident reports are never made public.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We share data only with trusted infrastructure and service partners strictly necessary to operate the platform:

  • Payment Gateway: Secure processing of online down payments, full payments, balances, and refunds.
  • Vercel & MonsterASP.NET: Web application deployment, server hosting, and database storage for platform records and operations.
  • Communication Delivery: Transactional notifications and verification alerts.

5. Automated Rules & Booking Status

  • Unconfirmed Bookings: Unpaid bookings or down payments expire automatically after the configured reservation window.
  • Completion & Settlement: If down payments were made, balances must be settled on or before the event date. Service completion and earnings releases are subject to administrative hold if a client files an immediate non-appearance or dispute report.

6. Security & Your Rights

  • Security: We apply role-based access controls, password hashing, and secure protocols to protect your personal and business records.
  • Your Rights: Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act, you have the right to access, rectify, object to processing, or request deletion of your personal data, and lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

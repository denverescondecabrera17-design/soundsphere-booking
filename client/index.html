FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions & Answers

What is SoundSphere?

SoundSphere is a web-based capstone project that connects clients with lights and sounds service providers. It allows clients to browse service packages, check availability, and make online payments.

How can I book a service?

Create an account, select services from your chosen service provider, choose an available date, and complete the online payment. Your booking will be confirmed after successful payment and verification of schedule availability.

What payment methods are available?

SoundSphere supports online payments through its configured payment gateway. Depending on the available booking options, clients may pay a down payment or the full amount online.

When should I pay the remaining balance?

If you choose a down payment, the remaining balance must be paid online on or before the scheduled event date, according to the booking terms.

Can I receive a refund after cancelling?

Refund eligibility depends on the applicable cancellation timeframe and the platform's refund policy. Cancellations made outside the eligible timeframe may not qualify for a refund.

How can I become a service provider?

Create a client account and submit an application for service provider approval. Provide the required personal and business information, valid identification, business permit, and other required details. You may subscribe to a provider plan after your application is approved.

How can I contact a service provider?

You can use SoundSphere's messaging feature to communicate with a service provider through text messages.
